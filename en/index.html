From industrial controllers to AI agents — I design, build and ship complete systems, not just code. Four disciplines, one mindset: understand the problem deeply, then build the simplest thing that solves it completely.

Full-Stack Web & App

Cross-platform, web-centric systems from pixel to production. Frontend with React, Vue, Angular and Next.js; backends with Node.js, Laravel, .NET, Django, FastAPI and Go; data layers on PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB and Redis — all shipped as one coherent product with Docker and CI/CD.

🤖

AI & Generative Systems

NLP and Generative AI in real production, not demos: LangChain pipelines, RAG knowledge bases, LLM API bridges and multi-agent orchestration. I wire models like GLM, Qwen, DeepSeek and Gemini into reliable OpenAI-compatible services that other software can build on.

PLC & Industrial Automation

Automation and process control programmed in Ladder Diagram (LD) and Structured Text (ST) under the IEC 61131-3 standard — from sensor and actuator logic to HMI-ready control programs that keep real machinery running safely, predictably and around the clock.

Research & Security

Applied research across cybersecurity, cognitive psychology and social engineering — plus history, logic and philosophy. Understanding how systems, networks and people actually behave is what lets me design software that survives contact with reality.
